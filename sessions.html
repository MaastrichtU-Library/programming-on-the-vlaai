🎯 Level Level 0 – Level 2

R for qualitative data – For total newbies

📅 When 29 October 2026, 10:00–13:00
📍 Where Maastricht University — location TBC
👥 For whom Researchers and students of Maastricht University
🎯 Level Level 0 – Level 2

Speaker: Dr. Szilvia Zörgő — Departement Metamedica, Faculty of Health, Medicine and Life Sciences, Maastricht University.

During this workshop, we will explore the fundamentals of working with textual data using the programming language R. We will explore use cases and do some digital experimentation ourselves, focused on topics such as qualitative coding and data segmentation, visualization, and analytical innovation. The workshop is designed for researchers who generally perform qualitative coding (by hand or with software) when working with their data, and would want to explore answers to the question: What is R and why would it be useful in my work?
